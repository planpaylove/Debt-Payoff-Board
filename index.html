How the maths works

Snowball attacks the smallest balance first — quick wins that keep you going. Avalanche attacks the highest interest rate first — the cheapest way out in pure maths.

Each month, interest is added first (balance × APR ÷ 12), then minimums are paid on every debt, then all remaining money in your monthly pool goes to the target debt. When a debt reaches zero, its minimum joins the attack the next month.

The debt-free date is the month the last balance hits zero. The What-If Simulator runs the exact same maths with a different extra payment or a one-time lump sum, so the dates and interest it shows are the same numbers your real plan would produce. Change a balance, a rate, or your extra payment and the whole plan re-works itself.
